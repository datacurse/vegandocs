Organisations​

The Vegan Society​

Veganism is a philosophy and way of living which seeks to exclude—as far as is possible and practicable—all forms of exploitation of, and cruelty to, animals for food, clothing or any other purpose; and by extension, promotes the development and use of animal-free alternatives for the benefit of animals, humans and the environment. In dietary terms it denotes the practice of dispensing with all products derived wholly or partly from animals.

Source: https://www.vegansociety.com/go-vegan/definition-veganism

Peta​

A vegan (strict vegetarian) does not consume meat, dairy products, eggs, honey, or any product derived from an animal. A vegan diet can (and should) be full of a wide variety of delicious, nutritious foods, including vegetables, grains, nuts, legumes, seeds, and fruits. Vegans don’t wear leather, fur, silk, or wool. Many refuse to use products that are made with animal ingredients, products that are filtered using animal parts (such as some wines, beers, and white sugars), and products that have been tested on animals.

Source: https://www.peta.org/about-peta/faq/what-is-a-vegan/

Viva​

Vegans don’t eat, wear or use anything from animals — whether from land animals (meat, dairy, eggs, honey, shellac, leather, fur etc) or from water animals (fish, prawns, crab, lobster etc). Vegans also exclude, as far as is practicable, all forms of animal exploitation and cruelty.

Source: https://viva.org.uk/lifestyle/definition-of-vegan/

National Cancer Institute​

A person who does not eat any foods that come from animals, including meat, eggs, and dairy products. A vegan diet is being studied in the prevention and treatment of prostate cancer and other medical conditions.

Source: https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/vegan

Dictionaries​

Wikipedia​

Veganism is the practice of abstaining from the use of animal products and the consumption of animal source foods, and an associated philosophy that rejects the commodity status of animals. A person who practices veganism is known as a vegan.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veganism

Merriam-Webster​

  • A strict vegetarian who consumes no food (such as meat, eggs, or dairy products) that comes from animals.
  • One who abstains from using animal products (such as leather).

Source: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/vegan

Cambridge Dictionary​

  • The practice of not eating or using any animal products, such as meat, fish, eggs, cheese, or leather.
  • Strict veganism prohibits the use of all animal products, not just food, and is a lifestyle choice rather than a diet.

Source: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/veganism

Collins​

  • Someone who is vegan never eats meat or any animal products such as milk, butter, or cheese.
  • A vegan is someone who is vegan.
  • A person who refrains from using any animal product whatever for food, clothing, or any other purpose.
  • A vegetarian who eats no animal products.
  • A vegetarian who omits all animal products from the diet.

Source: https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/vegan

Oxford Learners Dictionaries​

A person who does not eat any animal products such as meat, milk or eggs or use animal products such as leather or wool.

Source: https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/vegan_1?q=vegan

Britannica​

Veganism, the theory or practice of abstaining from the consumption and use of animal products.

Source: https://www.britannica.com/topic/veganism

Vocabulary.com​

  • Veganism is the philosophy and practice of eating no food that comes from animals, including meat, milk, and eggs. For some, veganism also means avoiding the use of animal products like leather and fur.
  • Veganism is the strictest form of vegetarianism, and people who follow this practice only eat plant-based foods. If you want to give veganism a try, you'll need to give up butter, cheese, and eggs — but there are plenty of substitutes made from soy, nuts, and other vegan sources. Vegan was coined in 1944, using the first three and last two letters of vegetarian, by Donald Watson, publisher of a newsletter for strict vegetarians called The Vegan News.
  • Veganism is the practice of eating only plant-based foods and sometimes avoiding the use of animal-based products.

Source: https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/veganism
